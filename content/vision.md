---
title: Our Vision
description: A swing dance festival in London, England
---

Following on from the feedback from our 2026 edition, while continuing to be anchored in the values of community, accessibility of knowledge, and celebration of the different facets of the Black art form we participate in, we are solidifying our artistic focus to the following trio of idea.

The Jazz Jam was magical and we want more of it for more people. For 2027 it will be more longer in terms of running time, with no conflicting classes. We will also ensure that it is included in any ticket for the event, from single day pass to full weekend.

The history of the dance and our Jazz Icons will be more front and centre. Rather than a jazz icon per teacher, it will be per day - allowing you to get a deeper slice of understanding rather than a quick spread of it.

London is our home and we want to showcase more of it. We are working on ways to have our elders and special guests more involved, and will be surfacing more of the history of our dance community in London and nationwide.
